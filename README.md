A make.fun entry for [One Line, One Chill](https://make.fun/bounty/u-azazex-one-line-one-chill-6d4d5488): one line of horror, under 200 characters, that has to frighten a reader in one breath.

> I checked the security footage to see who keeps opening my fridge at 3 a.m. and watched myself do it, smiling, while I was asleep in bed.

`main.py` (stdlib only) writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` and verifies it byte-for-byte on read-back.
